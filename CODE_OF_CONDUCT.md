# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Peregrine to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respecting different opinions and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of behavior that is not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as an address or email, without their
  explicit permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may
remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't
follow this Code of Conduct, and will explain why when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews) and when someone officially represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer,
[@Salim8519](https://github.com/Salim8519), through GitHub. If the report involves a
security problem, use the private form in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). All reports are
reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

## Enforcement guidelines

1. **Correction:** for inappropriate language or other minor unprofessional behavior. A
   private, written warning explaining what was wrong. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning:** for a single serious incident or repeated minor ones. No interaction with
   the people involved for a set time. Breaking this may lead to a ban.
3. **Temporary ban:** for a serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behavior.
   No interaction with the project for a set time.
4. **Permanent ban:** for a pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression toward or
   disparagement of groups of people.
